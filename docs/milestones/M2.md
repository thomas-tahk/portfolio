## How to try it
Open https://thomas-tahk.github.io/portfolio/preview/ on your phone and scroll top to bottom.

Watch for:
- Featured project buttons (Live / Code) open the right place.
- "Copy email" copies the address.
- The "Recent work" list near the bottom (see decision 1).
- Switch your phone to dark mode once and look again.
- "Play the game version →" at the very bottom.
