## How to try it
Open https://thomas-tahk.github.io/portfolio/preview/ on your phone and scroll top to bottom.

Watch for:
- Tap the ⚙ gear (top-right, beside "Game version →"). Try Dark mode, each palette, "Next palette", and "Auto-rotate" (changes palette every 8s).
- Reload: your choices stay.
- "Game version →" opens the game; "← Plain version" brings you back.
- Featured project buttons (Live / Code), "Copy email", and the "Recent work" list near the bottom.
